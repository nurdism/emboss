    Artwork

    Browse every Iconify set, then copy an icon ID such as mdi:home and paste it above.

    Anything in the box becomes the artwork, in place of an icon. Empty it to put the icon back. Heavier error correction shrugs off a rough print but needs more modules for the same text, so each one comes out smaller.

    Plate
    Border

    A rim following the plate outline, printed in the artwork colour at the artwork depth. Zero width turns it off. Mounting holes move inside it.

    Depth

    Mounting holes

    A hole normally gives up rather than land on the artwork or the rim. Cut through everything bores it anyway, taking a ring of the artwork with it so the two colours never meet at the hole edge. A hole that would fall off the plate is still dropped.

    View

    Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The buttons on the canvas fit the view, look straight down, and turn the measurements on or off.

    Copy link puts the whole sign in a URL, artwork included. An uploaded font file is too large to travel with it.
